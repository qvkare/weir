What 69 weeks cannot tell you: a share measured on this many weeks has the wide interval shown above, so "95%" here is consistent with a true rate several points lower. A one-in-a-hundred-weeks move has a 50% chance of not appearing at all in 69 weeks, so the worst move here is not the worst move possible.

## What this does not show

- **No real order flow.** Nobody traded these weeks. The clearing price is assumed (at the anchor, or shifted ±2% and ±5%); a real auction could clear further away, or not at all in a quiet week.
- **Asks and rate cards, not trades.** The index is the median of providers' posted on-demand prices. Posted prices are sticky: a provider can keep a list price for months while discounts, availability and negotiated deals move underneath. Low measured volatility partly reflects that stickiness, not a calm market. Where it overlaps (91 days from 2026-08-12) the composite sits on average 28% above Ornn's transaction-based H100 index (`data/derived/validation.json`): this is a list-price index, and a fixing on transaction prices would sit elsewhere and might move differently.
- **Sample length.** The sources reach back to 2024-11-01, but before 2025-04-01 fewer than 4 market providers are in the data, so the index (and the backtest) covers 2025-04-01 to 2026-11-10: 69 usable weeks, one gentle decline and one sharp rise. The 2024 to early-2025 price fall is not in the sample. Tail estimates (the 95% band) rest on a handful of weeks.
- **Provider survivorship and composition.** The composite uses whoever is in the sources on a day: the set changed on 243 index days. Providers enter (Akash from 2025-07, Cudo and Vast.ai from 2026-01, others in 2026-10) and change their SKU mix; the median can step when the set changes, without any provider changing price. Providers that left the market before or during the window are not represented.
- **Gaps.** Days without at least 4 providers have no index value and are never filled. Weeks with fewer than 4 daily values or a stale anchor are dropped, and dropped weeks may not be random (scrapes fail more often when things change).
- **Daily prints, not the on-chain cadence.** The contract may print more often than once a day; one daily snapshot is a coarse stand-in, taken at whatever hour the source ran.
- **Usage and fees.** Flat usage of 8 GPUs every hour; no fees, no cost of locked capital, no bonus pool, no forfeits.
- **Hyperscalers are excluded from the index** and appear only in the basis-risk table.
